## What changes

The site still reads as « sites, agents et automatisations », sites first. The pivot puts the order the other way round:

1. **Lead:** Klyx embeds an engineer in your team who ships AI agents, workflows and automations into production (forward-deployed engineering).
2. **Second:** fixed-scope AI work for teams that do not want someone embedded (Audit IA, Sprint IA, Automatisation IA).
3. **Add-on:** landing pages and sites, sold as « et la page qui le vend », no longer a headline offer.

Prices, durations and offer ids do not change. No new claim, client name or number.
