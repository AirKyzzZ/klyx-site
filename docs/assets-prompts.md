# Backdrop prompts (Gemini 3 Pro Image via OpenRouter)

Run each with the nano-banana-pro-openrouter skill, `--resolution 2K`, once `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` is set.

Shared constraints appended to every prompt: no text, no letters, no logos, no people's faces, no figures in the foreground, very low contrast, nothing bright in the centre, large calm dark areas for UI windows to sit on.

## 1. Quays at dusk (16:9)

A dark, warm, low-contrast oil painting of the Bordeaux quays along the Garonne at dusk, seen from across the river. Stone facades as soft silhouettes, a faint band of burnt orange (#F54E00) glow on the horizon reflected in the water, sand-grey haze, warm black (#141414) sky and foreground. Painterly brushwork, slightly out of focus, matte, wide 16:9. Large empty dark areas in the centre.

## 2. Pont de pierre and river (16:9)

A moody, painterly view of the Pont de pierre arches over the Garonne at twilight, almost monochrome warm black with sand-grey highlights and a single subtle ember-orange light low on the water. Soft mist, long horizontal reflections, heavy atmosphere, very low contrast, wide 16:9, dark upper two thirds.

## 3. Portrait crop for mobile (9:16)

A vertical, dark painterly landscape of the Garonne at dusk: a faint line of stone quays at the bottom, a wide calm river, a smoky sand-grey sky fading to warm black (#141414) at the top, one thin burnt orange (#F54E00) glow at the horizon. Soft brushstrokes, low contrast, matte, 9:16, generous dark space in the upper half for text.
