## The need

A high-end watchmaking workshop needs to know at any moment where every watch it is trusted with stands, from intake to return.

## What we built

Cronolyss, the workshop's internal software. It tracks each watch step by step, along with quotes, stock and subcontractors. We handled the interface, as part of a team: dashboard, repair records, intake with webcam photos, activity feed.

It is a private application, so there is no public link. Business software, no AI component.

## The result

The team sees at a glance what's moving and what's stuck.
