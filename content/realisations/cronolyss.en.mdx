Cronolyss is the internal software of a high-end watchmaking workshop: it tracks each watch from intake to return, along with quotes, stock and subcontractors. We handled the interface, as part of a team: dashboard, repair records, intake with webcam photos, activity feed. It is a private application, so there is no public link. Business software, no AI component.
