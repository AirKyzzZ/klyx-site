## The need

Know whether AI search engines cite a brand when a customer asks them a question.

## What we built

CiteMe queries AI search engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), tracks a brand's citations over time and shows what to fix in its content.

Maxime Mansiet, the founder of Klyx, co-founded it, so this is not a client project.

## The result

Brands see where they're cited and what to fix to be cited more.
