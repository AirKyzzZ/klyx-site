## The need

Hop Hop Immo tracks new-build programmes across south-west France, from Bordeaux to Toulouse, with their full price history. On the site, you look for a new home the way you would on a property portal: a town, a département on the map, or an area you draw yourself. Buyers needed help finding their way through all those programmes.

## What we built

Hoppy, the site's AI assistant, from design to launch. It adds another way to search: describe what you are looking for in your own words, out loud or in writing. It answers visitors' questions and points them to the right programme.

### What visitors see

1. On the home page, next to the regular search, a "Rechercher avec Hoppy" (search with Hoppy) button.
2. The button opens the assistant page: the map on one side, Hoppy on the other, and a "Critères de recherche" (search criteria) panel to fill in by talking.
3. Visitors choose to talk to Hoppy or to write to it.
4. Before voice mode, a window explains that the voice is processed by ElevenLabs, a provider based in the United States, and that recordings are not kept after processing. Visitors can accept or decline.

### The tools

The live site runs on Next.js. Hoppy's voice goes through ElevenLabs, as the site tells its visitors. The map shows OpenStreetMap data.

## The result

Buyers get an answer right away, without waiting for an advisor.
