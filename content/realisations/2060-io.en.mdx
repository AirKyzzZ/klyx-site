## The need

2060 builds the infrastructure that lets humans, services and AI agents prove who they are. To show that trust layer, it needed agents, demos and a mobile app.

## What we built

Maxime Mansiet, the founder of Klyx, consults on its Hologram network:

- a multilingual travel concierge, orchestrated over MCP servers;
- a live video avatar;
- in the mobile app, the screen that shows users a service's proof of trust before the first connection.
