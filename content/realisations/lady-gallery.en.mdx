## The need

Lady Gallery is a student project from the language department of the University of Bordeaux: a day dedicated to English-speaking women artists. It needed a website to present the event, in French and English.

## What we built

A bilingual website that presents the event, the artists and their biographies, the workshops and the partnerships. Web only, no AI component.
