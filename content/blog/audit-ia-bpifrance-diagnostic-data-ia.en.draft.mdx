## What is Bpifrance's Diagnostic Data IA?

The Diagnostic Data IA is support from an expert who helps an SME or mid-sized company find where AI can be useful. It covers a first technical and operational review, concrete use cases, and their ranking by added value ([Bpifrance press office](https://presse.bpifrance.fr/renforcement-du-plan-national-osez-lia-pour-sensibiliser-les-entreprises-a-lintelligence-artificielle-et-les-accompagner-dans-sa-mise-en-oeuvre/?lang=fra), 17 June 2026). It's part of the Osez l'IA plan, run by Bpifrance Conseil on behalf of the French state ([entreprises.gouv.fr](https://www.entreprises.gouv.fr/osez-lia), updated 28 September 2026).

The diagnostic doesn't build anything: it produces recommendations, and implementation has to be funded separately.

## How much does the Diagnostic Data IA cost?

The Diagnostic Data IA costs €10,000 excl. VAT, with 40% covered by France 2030 ([entreprises.gouv.fr](https://www.entreprises.gouv.fr/osez-lia), updated 28 September 2026). That leaves €6,000 excl. VAT for the company. This amount appears on the [Bpifrance catalogue page](https://www.bpifrance.fr/catalogue-offres/diag-data-ia), which we could only read through a search engine on 3 November 2026.

Older amounts are still in circulation, and we come back to them below. Before signing, get the amount confirmed in your own application.

## Which companies are eligible for the Diagnostic Data IA?

Eligible companies are SMEs and mid-sized companies with 10 to 2,000 employees registered with the French RCS ([Bpifrance press office](https://presse.bpifrance.fr/renforcement-du-plan-national-osez-lia-pour-sensibiliser-les-entreprises-a-lintelligence-artificielle-et-les-accompagner-dans-sa-mise-en-oeuvre/?lang=fra), 17 June 2026). Les-aides.fr adds two conditions: at least €1M revenue on a 12-month balance sheet and more than a year in business ([les-aides.fr](https://les-aides.fr/aide/aVkf3w/bpifrance/diagnostic-data-intelligence-artificielle.html), updated 12 August 2026). Both also appear on the Bpifrance catalogue page as seen through a search engine, but we couldn't read the page itself. Check with your regional Bpifrance office if you're close to a threshold.

## How long does the Diagnostic Data IA take?

The Diagnostic Data IA is 8 person-days ([entreprises.gouv.fr](https://www.entreprises.gouv.fr/osez-lia), updated 28 September 2026). Les-aides.fr spreads them over 3 months at most ([les-aides.fr](https://les-aides.fr/aide/aVkf3w/bpifrance/diagnostic-data-intelligence-artificielle.html), updated 12 August 2026).

## How does the diagnostic run, and how do you apply?

The process goes through a Bpifrance-accredited expert ([Bpifrance white paper](https://diag.bpifrance.fr/assets/documents/Livre_blanc%20_Diag_Data_IA_24_09_10.pdf), 2024), who first checks with you that the diagnostic makes sense. The 2023 specifications describe a pre-qualification interview, then a commercial proposal, and say funding stays subject to the application being accepted ([Bpifrance](https://diaginno.bpifrance.fr/storage/sites/2/2023/07/Diagnostic-Data-IA-Cahier-des-charges.pdf), 2023 PDF). The same document describes three phases:

1. Assess the company's maturity and its priority areas for creating value.
2. Scope the project and identify one or more use cases to test.
3. Define an action plan on data collection, structure and governance.

The expert delivers a final report with a summary, meeting notes and the action plan (same source). Terms have changed since 2023: the document's amounts are out of date, and the process may have moved too. The official portal is [diag.bpifrance.fr](https://diag.bpifrance.fr/diag-data-ia).

## Why do figures differ from one page to the next?

Figures differ because the scheme has changed and older pages are still online. The [June 2023 specifications](https://diaginno.bpifrance.fr/storage/sites/2/2023/07/Diagnostic-Data-IA-Cahier-des-charges.pdf) announced €3,900 to €13,000 excl. VAT for 3 to 10 days, and other old sheets give other rates again. The most recent official sources we could read, [Bpifrance's press release of 17 June 2026](https://presse.bpifrance.fr/renforcement-du-plan-national-osez-lia-pour-sensibiliser-les-entreprises-a-lintelligence-artificielle-et-les-accompagner-dans-sa-mise-en-oeuvre/?lang=fra) and the [ministry page updated 28 September 2026](https://www.entreprises.gouv.fr/osez-lia), give €10,000 excl. VAT, 40% and 8 person-days. Those are what we go with. If a provider quotes you something else, ask for their source and its date.

## Does the Diagnostic Data IA also fund implementation?

No, the Diagnostic Data IA funds the diagnostic and not the development that follows. The 2023 specifications say later steps, such as a feasibility study or the project launch, may where applicable fall under Bpifrance's innovation aid ([Bpifrance](https://diaginno.bpifrance.fr/storage/sites/2/2023/07/Diagnostic-Data-IA-Cahier-des-charges.pdf), 2023 PDF). Nothing is automatic, and you should check that aid when you apply. So set aside a separate budget to build what the diagnostic recommends.

## How many diagnostics have been started?

Bpifrance reported 70 diagnostics started and planned to roll out 1,000 in the plan's new phase ([Bpifrance press office](https://presse.bpifrance.fr/un-an-du-plan-osez-lia-un-premier-bilan-positif-et-une-nouvelle-etape-pour-accelerer-ladoption-de-lia-dans-les-entreprises/?lang=fra), 8 September 2026). France 2030 is putting €9.6M into them ([Bpifrance press office](https://presse.bpifrance.fr/renforcement-du-plan-national-osez-lia-pour-sensibiliser-les-entreprises-a-lintelligence-artificielle-et-les-accompagner-dans-sa-mise-en-oeuvre/?lang=fra), 17 June 2026).

## Is there other support for larger companies?

Yes, the Accélérateurs IA are aimed at larger companies: over €8M revenue, more than 50 staff and at least 3 years in business. The programme runs 18 months, with a 12-day diagnostic and two 13-day modules, and France 2030 covers 46% of the cost ([entreprises.gouv.fr](https://www.entreprises.gouv.fr/osez-lia), updated 28 September 2026).
